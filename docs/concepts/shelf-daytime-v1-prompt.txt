Use case: ui-mockup
Asset type: A first visual study for Caleb Li's personal website, desktop homepage in daytime mode. This is an art-direction mockup to review before implementing a site, not a wireframe.

Primary request: "Smiskis on a Shelf": an imagined small LUSTIGT-inspired pale wooden wall shelf populated with Smiski collectible figures and meaningful personal objects. The shelf is the centerpiece and acts as navigation into About, Projects, Interests, and Contact. A personal home on the web, with a professional portfolio as a major part of it.

Scene/backdrop: A softly lit warm off-white plaster wall. One coherent open shelf assembly with three staggered pale wooden platforms and slim wooden upright supports. Mostly frontal camera with a very slight downward and side angle so platform depth is visible and the objects remain easy to recognize. Soft contact shadows and restrained natural wood grain.

Subjects and composition: Wide landscape desktop website composition with plenty of breathing room. Small restrained text navigation along the top. Introductory typography on the left, the shelf taking the center and right of the page. Arrange the figures and objects at a readable scale, with varied heights, small clusters, intentional empty space, and clear separation between scenes. The top scene contains a seated Smiski beside a small framed photo, angled so the photograph is indistinct and no identifiable portrait of Caleb is invented. The middle scene has a miniature open laptop with one Smiski studying it and another lying exhausted nearby. The bottom scene contains a colorful Rubik's cube and a tennis ball, with one figure leaning on the cube and another gently touching the ball. A small envelope with a nearby peeking figure is the contact element. Keep the shelf structurally plausible, with every object resting on a wooden platform. No extra decorative props.

Character design: Recognizable Smiski-like small pale green collectible figures with simple round oversized heads, tiny black dot eyes, no hair, no clothes, smooth rounded bodies and limbs, soft matte tactile translucent resin. Charming natural sitting, peeking, leaning, and resting poses. The figures are collectible miniatures, not realistic human anatomy. Avoid exaggerated facial expressions, mouths, noses, or cartoon outlines.

Style/medium: High-quality miniature product render integrated into a refined, simple website. Soft and tactile, welcoming, specific, lived in, a little playful. Convincing sculptural forms and wood materials. No flat vector illustration. Daylight only, no night glow yet. Crisp readable humanist sans-serif website typography, modest supporting text, dark warm charcoal ink, generous negative space. The website interface should stay quiet so the objects provide the personality.

Text (verbatim): Top left small wordmark "Caleb Li". Top navigation "About   Projects   Interests   Contact". Left introductory heading "Hey, I'm Caleb.". Supporting text "Welcome to my little shelf of things I make and care about.". Small prompt "Pick something to explore". Discreet scene labels "About me", "Projects", "Interests", and "Contact" placed close to their corresponding objects. No other text.

Constraints: One complete daytime homepage mockup, wide desktop aspect ratio. All figures, props, shelf edges, labels, and introductory text fully inside the image. Preserve the balance between a personal miniature scene and readable website navigation. No browser chrome, device frames, logos on the laptop, watermark, gradients on text, cards surrounding the sections, dashboard widgets, plants, mugs, books, extra hobby objects, or unrelated decoration.
